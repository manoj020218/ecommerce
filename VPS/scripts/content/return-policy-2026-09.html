Return & Replacement Policy

Effective {{effectiveDate}}

{{storeName}} sells security, IoT and electronic products at wholesale prices. To keep prices low, most products are sold on a "no return" basis. Returns are accepted only for products clearly marked as return-eligible, and only for manufacturing defects found on arrival. We offer replacement only — no cash refunds.

1. Which products can be returned

  • Return-eligible products show "✅ Return eligible — replacement if defective on arrival (5 days)" on the product page, in the cart and at checkout.
  • All other products show "⚠️ Sold as is — not returnable". Many are low-cost trading items supplied as received from the manufacturer or distributor, without testing, so that we can offer them at wholesale prices. These cannot be returned, replaced or refunded.
  • The marking shown on the product page when you place your order is final. If you are unsure, please contact us before ordering.

2. When a replacement is accepted

A replacement is accepted only when all of the following are true:

  1. The product is marked return eligible.
  2. It is defective on arrival — it does not power on or does not work as described when first used — because of a manufacturing defect.
  3. You report it within 5 days of delivery.
  4. You provide an unboxing video: one continuous, unedited video starting from the sealed parcel with the shipping label visible. Claims without an unboxing video will not be accepted.
  5. The product is returned complete — original box, accessories, manuals, cables and adapters — with serial number / barcode stickers intact.
  6. It is not physically damaged, and has not been altered, opened for repair, re-flashed or modified.

3. What is not covered

  • Any product not marked return eligible (sold as is).
  • Change of mind, wrong model or quantity ordered, lower price found elsewhere, or no longer needed.
  • Incompatibility with your existing system, or unsuitability for your site or application.
  • Damage from installation or use: wrong wiring, wrong voltage or adapter, power surge or lightning, water or moisture, burns, drilling or mounting marks, or physical damage after delivery.
  • Configuration, network, app, password or software issues — these are not defects, and we are happy to help you set up.
  • Normal wear and tear, scratches or cosmetic marks from use.
  • Consumables (batteries, cards, tags, cables cut to length) and custom-printed or special-order items.
  • Products reported after 5 days from delivery, or without an unboxing video.

4. How to request a replacement

  1. Within 5 days of delivery, contact us on WhatsApp / phone {{phone}} or email {{email}} with your order number, the product name and serial number, a description of the problem, photos and a short video of the defect, and your unboxing video.
  2. We review your request, usually within 2 working days, and may do a remote check (for example a video call) before approving it.
  3. If approved, you receive a Return Authorisation (RMA) number and our return address. Parcels sent without an RMA number will be refused.
  4. Pack the product securely in its original packaging, mark the RMA number on it, and ship it within 5 days of approval.

5. Shipping costs

  • Return shipping is paid by the buyer, including courier and packing.
  • The original delivery charges are not refundable.
  • Returns lost or damaged in transit are the buyer's responsibility — please use a trackable courier and keep the receipt.
  • If we sent you the wrong item, we will bear the return shipping (see section 7).

6. Inspection and replacement

  • We inspect every returned product, usually within 5 working days of receipt.
  • If a manufacturing defect is confirmed, we will replace the product with the same item — or, if it is no longer available, an equivalent item of equal value — and ship it to you. We do not offer cash refunds.
  • If no defect is found, or the damage is excluded under section 3, the product will be returned to you at your cost, or held for 15 days, after which it may be disposed of.

7. Damaged or wrong item received

  • If the parcel arrives visibly damaged or tampered with, please refuse it, or report it with photos and your unboxing video within 48 hours of delivery.
  • If you received a different product from the one you ordered, report it within 48 hours with your unboxing video. We will arrange the correct item and bear the return shipping.

8. Manufacturer warranty (after 5 days)

After the 5-day replacement window, warranty claims for branded products are handled according to the manufacturer's warranty terms. We will help you contact the brand or service centre, but repair time, service and shipping to the service centre are governed by the manufacturer. Unbranded or "sold as is" products carry no warranty unless the product page states otherwise.

9. Cancellations

  • Before dispatch: you may cancel free of charge. Payments already made are refunded to the original payment method (payment gateway charges, if any, are not refundable).
  • After dispatch: orders cannot be cancelled; this policy applies.

10. Business (B2B / GST) orders

This policy applies equally to business and GST-invoice orders. Replacements are documented against the original tax invoice.
